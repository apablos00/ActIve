## Product principles
1. **Ask before telling.** The first move on every concept is a question.
2. **Hint ladder.** Nudge, then partial hint. Explain only after three real attempts. Then re-test.
3. **Short turns.** One question per turn. Two sentences max. Silence is fine.
4. **Bridge everything.** Every concept links to the student's hobby or career goal.
5. **Minimalist UI.** One focus per screen. One accent color per mentor.
6. **No real-person voices.** Mentors are archetypes.

## Not in the MVP
Teacher UI, extra agents, analytics dashboards, phone calls, voice cloning, multi-language, databases, auth.

## Roadmap slide only
Skeptic voice (agent transfer). Class analytics (conversation analysis). Exam-eve phone drill (Twilio). Professor's consented voice. WhatsApp drills.
