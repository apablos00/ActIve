## Q&A
- **"Isn't this ChatGPT with a voice?"** ChatGPT is built to answer. We are built to question.
- **"Privacy? A Chinese model?"** The model layer is swappable. Production runs on EU-hosted endpoints.
- **"Why a hologram?"** Students study with someone they look up to, not a chat box.
- **"Why voice?"** Talking forces retrieval. You can't copy-paste a conversation.
